Solid Rock

Standalone notification ecosystem, scrolling text, and custom ringtones (uses the same as your phone) for your smartwatch.

⚙️ System Requirements:
Compatible with **Wear OS 5 and newer** watches (e.g., updated Galaxy Watch 4/5/6/7/Ultra, Pixel Watch 2/3) paired with **Android 8 or higher** phones. Not compatible with iPhone, Tizen, or HarmonyOS.
ON THE WATCH FACE
⌚
• Large clock (including LED Board and Stylus styles), date, sender picture or app icon (Outlook, Wallet, Gmail…). • Scrolling notification text (sender and message, "1 of 2"). • Complete weather row: temperatures, rain, feels like, humidity, pressure with trend, wind, UV, visibility, snow, air quality, and pollen. • Watch battery, steps, alarm, and calendar events; quick icons: microphone, custom app shortcut, settings.
MESSAGES
💬
• Message page with picture right below the name (zoom), pill-styled chat history, and navigation arrows ‹ ›. • Quick replies, dictation, keyboard, voice typing; option to reply again to already answered messages. • Previews for PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, CSV, and RTF; video playback; text-to-speech reading. • Automatic WhatsApp voice note transcription; APK files received in chat: "📦 Install on Watch". • Direct Telegram connection (two accounts): native replies and voice notes; download photos, videos, and APKs without opening the phone app.
CALLS
📞
• Calls trigger directly from the phone ring: name, contact ringtone, Answer / Decline. • Full-screen contact picture as background, buttons in the foreground. • Optional: Set Solid Rock as the watch default Phone app (duration, mute, audio output, volume, dialer keypad). • WhatsApp calls integration with Answer / Decline buttons.
RINGTONES AND VOLUMES
🎵
• Ringtones per app, per contact, and for calls; syncs with phone contact settings; personal audio files up to 10 MB. • Dedicated vibrations, night silence mode, respect for phone's Do Not Disturb. • Four volume controls: notifications, calls, text-to-speech, and watch alarms (also boosts assistant voice).
CONTROLLER AND WIDGET
📱
• Numbered menu with expandable submenus (a., b., c.); STATUS box showing both watch and phone versions. • Complete permission page: tap any ✓/✗ row to jump straight to system settings. • Phone widget: watch battery status, WhatsApp and Telegram accounts status, next alarm, "Find Watch", text speed, and volumes.
ADDITIONAL FEATURES
🚀
• SR Assistant 🔒 FULL : tap the clock and speak: messages, calls, music controls, timer, alarms, "open app...", Google Gemini AI questions. • Watch alarms configured from the phone, backup phone alarm if watch is charging, ice-alert (advances alarm if under 0°C), find phone. • Full-screen weather views (Current · 24 Hours · 7 Days) with risk level colors; Battery and Health pages (steps from midnight). • FREE and FULL options (15-day free trial); optional anonymous statistics; 7 languages supported.
GOOD TO KNOW
⚠️
• Requires a Wear OS 5 or newer smartwatch and an Android smartphone. • After updating to version 212, re-apply the watch face (switch to another face and then back to Solid Rock). • Direct WhatsApp integration is unofficial: WhatsApp may temporarily block the number.
